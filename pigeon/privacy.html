Pigeon Mail — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Pigeon Mail ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial macOS mail client used by its author. This policy explains how the app handles data.

What the app accesses

With your permission, the app connects to your Google account (Gmail API, scope gmail.modify) and/or your Microsoft account (Microsoft Graph) to read, organize, archive, and send your email.

Where data is stored

  • Your messages are stored only on your Mac, in the app's local database.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only in your Mac's Keychain.
  • The app has no server. The developer does not collect, receive, store, or sell your data.

Sharing

Your data is not shared with anyone, with two exceptions that you control:

  • Sender logos (optional, can be turned off in Settings): the app may download a sender's website icon from that sender's own domain, which reveals your IP address to that website.
  • AI reply drafts (optional, off by default): only when you click "Draft with AI" in a reply, the email you are replying to is sent to Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (Codex) through your own signed-in command-line tool, to produce a draft you review before sending. Nothing is sent automatically.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the app's mail features to you and is not used for advertising.

Removing your data

Remove an account in the app's Settings to delete its local mail and tokens. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or account.live.com/consent/Manage (Microsoft).
